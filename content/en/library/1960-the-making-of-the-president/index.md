A two-player card-driven strategy game recreating the 1960 US presidential race between John F. Kennedy and Richard Nixon. Players campaign across a map of the era's electoral votes, building momentum on the key issues of the day while historical event cards, from the televised debates to Eisenhower's endorsement, shape the course of the election.
