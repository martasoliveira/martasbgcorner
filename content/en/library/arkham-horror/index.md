A cooperative adventure game inspired by H. P. Lovecraft and set in the town of Arkham in 1926. Investigators move around the streets, gather skills, allies and equipment, and venture into other worlds to close the gates that keep opening across town. If too many gates stay open, the chosen Ancient One awakens and the investigators must face it in a final battle.
