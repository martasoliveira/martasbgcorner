A tableau-building card game in which you are the last heirs of a great ancient civilisation, trying to preserve your people's knowledge. Monuments from across the ancient world, from the Sphinx of Giza to the temples of Tiwanaku, rise on your board and gradually slide into the past, and the knowledge attached to them is lost unless you act in time. Chaining monument effects with Technology cards is the key to a strong engine.
