A worker placement game set before an asteroid strikes the Earth. Each player leads one of four ideological Paths and uses workers, Exosuits and time travel to prepare for the impact and to shape what is left of the world afterwards. The Infinity Box brings together the base game, all the expansions and modules, and the Kickstarter extras.
