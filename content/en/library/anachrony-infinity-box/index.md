The complete Kickstarter edition of Anachrony, a worker-placement and tableau-building game set before an impending asteroid impact. Leaders of four ideological Paths use workers, Exosuits, and Time Rifts to prepare for the collision and dominate what's left of New Earth afterward. This edition bundles the base game with every expansion and module released, plus exclusive miniatures and stretch goals.
