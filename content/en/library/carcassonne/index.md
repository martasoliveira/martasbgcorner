A tile-laying classic in which players draw and place landscape tiles one at a time to build a medieval countryside of cities, roads, monasteries and fields. After placing a tile, a player may put one of their followers on one of its features, and it scores once that city, road or monastery is completed, while farmers score at the end of the game.
