An engine-building card game played over four rounds, each made up of a draft, a planning phase and a production phase. Players decide which cards to put into construction and which to recycle for resources, then produce resources in a fixed order to complete their constructions and grow their empire. Everyone plays simultaneously, so there is no waiting between turns.
