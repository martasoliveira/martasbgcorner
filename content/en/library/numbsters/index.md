A solo card game you can play in your hand or on a table, published in France as 8 Qui Croc'. Starting with a stack of six numbered cards plus the Mouth card, you rearrange the cards and feed them to each other in sequence, trying to whittle the stack down to a single card before the Mouth reaches the top and eats you.
