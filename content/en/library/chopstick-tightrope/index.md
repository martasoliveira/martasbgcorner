A dexterity party game where, on your turn, you draw a card and try to reproduce its pattern by placing meeples on an elastic rope, using only chopsticks. Succeed and you keep the card and its points; drop a meeple and the card is turned face down as a negative point. Special cards such as Force Second Card add a bit of risk and mischief.
