A modern classic worker-placement game where you and your spouse grow a farm from almost nothing, plowing fields, raising animals, building fences, and expanding your family, all while making sure everyone gets fed. Occupation and Minor Improvement cards make each game unfold differently depending on the strategy you draw into.
