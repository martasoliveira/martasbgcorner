An abstract tile-drafting game inspired by the Portuguese azulejos. Players take all the tiles of one colour from a shared display, place them on their pattern lines and move them to their wall at the end of each round, scoring for adjacent tiles and completed rows, columns and colours, and losing points for any tiles they cannot use.
