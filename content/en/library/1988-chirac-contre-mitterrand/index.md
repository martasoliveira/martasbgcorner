A short tactical card game set in French politics of the late 1980s, in which each side plays one of the two presidential candidates, Jacques Chirac or François Mitterrand. Players pick and resolve event cards inspired by the news of the time to win over public opinion through charisma, courage and integrity, while keeping enough power to avoid an early defeat.
