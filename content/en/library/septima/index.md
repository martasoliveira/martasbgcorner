Players are witches who have come to Noctenburg to prove they are worthy of becoming the next Septima, the leader of all witches. Over the Seasons they collect herbs, brew potions, heal the townsfolk, master charms and rescue their coven from the trials, while keeping suspicion in check: matching another player's action makes it stronger, but raises suspicion and risks drawing the Hunters. The Moon Phase decides which ingredients can be gathered.
