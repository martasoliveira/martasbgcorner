A deck-building card game in which you lead your kingdom from the dark ages to the age of enlightenment. Each turn gives you three actions to play cards, draw basic cards or draft from the Trade Route, and every card needs the right lands and people already in your kingdom. Reaching town centre and then castle level unlocks the intermediate and advanced decks, and the game ends once the Age of Enlightenment card appears. The box includes the base game and three mini expansions.
