A worker placement game set at the end of the Carolingian Empire, in which players are royal architects building landmarks to impress the king. Workers are placed in the city to gather materials, hire apprentices and construct buildings, while captured workers, the black market and taxes push players to make moral choices that are only judged at the end of the game.
