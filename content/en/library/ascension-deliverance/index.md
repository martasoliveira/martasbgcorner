A standalone set for the Ascension deck-building game, in which the boundary between dreams and reality has collapsed. Players buy heroes and constructs from a shared centre row to improve their decks, using new Phantasm cards that trigger effects from the centre row and Transform cards that upgrade cards already in play, as they race to close the portal to the Dreamscape.
