The second chapter of the Black Rose Wars series. Mages compete in a hexagonal arena to become Crown Holder of the rebuilt Lodge: each round they prepare spells on their board, then cast them in turn order to fight their rivals. A mage who falls simply returns to their room, so nobody is ever knocked out of the game.
