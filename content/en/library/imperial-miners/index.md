A light tableau-building card game set in the Imperial Settlers universe, where each player leads one of six dwarf factions digging their mine deeper, floor by floor. Placing a card sets off chain reactions through your mine, and climbing the progress tracks earns bonuses along the way, all in the race to collect the most Victory Points.
