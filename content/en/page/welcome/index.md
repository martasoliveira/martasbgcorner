## Hi, I'm Marta!

*You just scanned my t-shirt, so thank you for being curious!*

I'm a Portuguese girl who has loved board games for as long as I can remember. My favourites are Euro games and deckbuilders, and I play a lot of them solo. Here on **Marta's BG Corner** I share honest reviews, recaps of my plays and a peek into my collection, in English and French.

🎪 I'm at SPIEL Essen 2026!

This is my **first SPIEL ever**, and I'm there from **22 to 24 October**, wandering the halls with a huge smile and a bag that gets heavier by the hour.

### On my radar this year

I'll be honest: I haven't deep-dived into any of these yet. I'm following my gut, picking the games that caught my eye first, for their colours, their theme or their illustrations. After all, that's what my first SPIEL is about: discovering new games and seeing what's being made. And yes, they can all be played solo! 🎲

- **[Distilled: Cocktails](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/431123/distilled-cocktails)** (Paverson Games): I really enjoy Distilled and its theme, so a big expansion that adds cocktails to my distillery is an easy yes for my radar.
- **[Gaudí](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/457557/gaudi)** (Devir): the colours and the theme won me over straight away, and it's by Dani Garcia, a designer I'm really keen to explore. I've played his Coming of Age, and Daitoshi is waiting on my shelf to be tried.
- **[Queen Alice](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/456236/queen-alice)** (Combo Games): first the theme and the illustrations, then the way you choose your actions by moving pieces on a chessboard, with several boards offering different actions. I'm so curious to see it on the table.
- **[Timespan](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/462479/timespan)** (Alion – by Dr Ø): I love civilization games, but they're rarely great solo. A civ game that promises a good solo mode and a fairly quick play? Yes, please!
- **[The Dawn of Pangaea](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/449854/the-dawn-of-pangaea)** (Treeceratops): to be honest, it was the illustrations and the components that conquered me. I haven't read much about it, so it will be a surprise!

**Found a hidden gem at the fair?** Tell me on [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/@martasbgcorner) or [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61579559053699). I'd love to know what you discovered! I'll be posting my SPIEL adventures on both.
