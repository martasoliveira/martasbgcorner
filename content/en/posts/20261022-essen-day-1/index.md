Hello everyone!

This year I'm at SPIEL Essen for the very first time, and it's amazing! Let me take you along on a tour of my three days at the fair, starting with Day 1.

### Getting there

Today was the big day. I was super excited and a bit anxious at the same time, because I really didn't know what to expect.

We started the day in Bochum, where we've been staying for a few days. From Bochum, it's about a 20 minute train ride to Essen, then a short metro trip to the Messe. But when we arrived at Essen Central Station, surprise: a huge queue for the metro! We were waiting patiently, already resigned to a 30 minute wait, when a really nice person came up to us and asked if we wanted to share an Uber. We said yes, and we reached the Messe in 10 minutes. So thank you to that wonderful person whose name I unfortunately didn't ask. 😞

That's how our journey began: as a real adventure.

### First impressions

In front of the Messe, crowds of people were already heading inside, but getting in was quick and easy. Thank you to everyone organising and working at the event!

My first thought when I walked in was: oh my God, this is BIG. We started wandering between the booths, taking photos and trying not to collapse with so many things to look at, when someone asked us in French if we wanted to join a table to play Moustache. We said yes straight away, even though my boyfriend doesn't speak French. 😛

### Moustache

*Designed by [Alexandre Aguilar](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/141207/alexandre-aguilar) and [Jules Messaud](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/144139/jules-messaud) · Illustrated by [Manu Gorobeï](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgameartist/106300/manu-gorobei) · Published by [KODAMA](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamepublisher/61983/kodama-the-playful-spirit) · [BGG page](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/448428/moustache)*

Moustache is a trick-taking game with four animal colours. Each colour has a different strength, from green (the weakest) to blue (the strongest), and some cards have stars, which are the points you count up.

The fun part is that you play in teams, but the teams change every round and are randomly assigned. On top of that, a new rule card is revealed each round and stays active until the end, so the game keeps changing under your feet. The winning team of each round takes trophies, and after four rounds, the player with the most points wins.

We had the pleasure of playing with Vanessa and Leticia, a lovely Belgian mother and daughter. Before we even started, Vanessa announced, in a really funny way, that she was going to win. And guess what? She did! 😄 We laughed a lot and had a really great time. Thank you both, you were brilliant! The game really shone with you two, and thanks to that I can say I'd recommend Moustache to anyone who likes quick games.

### Gorilla Games: Chopstick Tightrope

*Published by [Gorilla Games](https://gorillagames.co.uk)*

Thank you to Oda By for the enthusiastic presentation of Chopstick Tightrope. It's a dexterity game where you build patterns on an elastic band, placing the pieces with chopsticks. It's so much fun! I haven't played a full game yet, but I already know it's going to bring a lot of laughs. In fact, I couldn't resist and it was my first purchase of the fair, and I was even offered [Cat Days](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/414274/cat-days) with it!

We also had a really interesting chat with Oda about board games and how important player feedback is. Thank you for your time, Oda!

### Black Beacon Games: Era of Tribes

*Designed by [Arne Lorenz](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/90041/arne-lorenz) · Illustrated by [Marc Exner](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgameartist/58107/marc-exner), [Radosław Jaszczuk](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgameartist/59030/radoslaw-jaszczuk) and Arne Lorenz · Published by [Black Beacon Games](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamepublisher/40481/black-beacon-games) · [BGG page](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/196354/era-of-tribes)*

At Black Beacon Games, we got a presentation of the rules of Era of Tribes, and I have to confess, it won me over much more than I expected. It's a civilisation game, and the box marks around 30 minutes per player.

The first thing that grabbed me was the big map of Europe. Then came the chance to really deploy your troops and go into battle. The actions feel realistic too: taxes, diplomacy, expansion, trade, revolution and more.

{{< figure
  src="era-of-tribes.jpg"

My boyfriend, who isn't that much into board games, was completely won over. He prefers games with a real theme, and this one sounded like an actual civilisation coming to life. That says a lot! I'll most likely be digging deeper into this one.

### Ice Makes: Epochs: Course of Cultures

*Designed by [Jeffrey CCH](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/104853/jeffrey-cch) · Published by [Ice Makes](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamepublisher/37978/ice-makes) · [BGG page](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/400817/epochs-course-of-cultures)*

At the Ice Makes booth, we discovered Epochs: Course of Cultures, another civilisation game that really appealed to me, especially its theme. It plays in around two hours at two players, which is really nice for me. I loved the look of it, with beautiful cards and a player board that reminded me a lot of [Age of Civilization](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/264647/age-of-civilization). And no wonder: both games are by the same designer, Jeffrey CCH!

### Devir: 7th Seal

*Designed by [Julio Falagán](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/171001/julio-falagan) · Illustrated by [Will Kirkby](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgameartist/47352/will-kirkby) · Published by [Devir](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamepublisher/2366/devir) · [BGG page](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/457575/7th-seal)*

I was expecting [Gaudí](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/457557/gaudi) to be the game that grabbed me at Devir, but no, it was 7th Seal! It's a solo game where you fight several bosses and their minions using dice. I got to try it until I beat the first boss, Teskatlipoka, and I was completely hooked.

### Pektis Studio: Battle of the Knollen and Fintastic Sea

*Published by [Pektis Studio](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamepublisher/52243/pektis-studio) · [Battle of the Knollen on BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/456557/battle-of-the-knollen) · [Fintastic Sea on BGG](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgame/442978/fintastic-sea)*

At Pektis Studio, we met [Echo](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/147577/xue-cui) and [Julian](https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgamedesigner/147576/julian-asleh), two of the three people behind this indie studio.

Echo presented Battle of the Knollen, a trick-taking game about potatoes designed by Julian. 😛 She showed it with so much passion, along with the beautiful illustrations she created, that it was hard not to fall in love with it. I'll be following the Gamefound campaign closely and waiting impatiently for its release.

### My favourite of the day

Without hesitation: Moustache. It was so light and fun, and it gave me so much happiness. Sometimes the best moment of a giant fair is simply sitting at a table with strangers and laughing together.

### End of Day 1

All in all, it was a great day. I'm tired, but I wanted so much to share it with you that I just couldn't wait.

Tomorrow I'll be back for more adventures: Fear of the Dark is on my list, along with more demos, and hopefully a notebook full of names this time!
